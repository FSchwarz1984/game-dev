# Manga marks (漫符, docs/design/dialogue-staging.md §2.1) as small pixel sprites with a few frames each, in the
# style of the marks drawn into the v4 portraits (tools/art/portraits4/edits/_symbols.txt): 1-px ink outline, white
# fill, the same reds and blues. Built by staging_fx.py. The blush lines and the gloom lines are not here: they are
# drawn for each face (staging_fx.py), since they must fit its eyes.
